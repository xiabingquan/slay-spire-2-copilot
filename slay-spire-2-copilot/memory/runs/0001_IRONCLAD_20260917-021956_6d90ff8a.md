## Run review

Classic Ironclad Vulnerable-payoff build executed well: starter Bash into
DISMANTLE x2, then UPPERCUT and THUNDERCLAP as extra Vulnerable enablers, then
the Bag of Marbles relic making every combat open with Vulnerable. Card-reward
and shop discipline thinned the deck (2 Strikes removed at Luminous Choir,
Spore Mind curse bought out at the shop) so combo pieces showed up on time.

The run died on HP economy, not card quality. Act 1's boss (Vantom) hit for
7/12/21/28 across its turns; even with a correct heal at the last rest site
(50→74) the boss left 1 HP. Burning Blood (+6 post-combat) was the only
recovery, and Act 2 opened with no heal. Block density stayed at 4 Defends —
never found a second block source — so any 15+ dmg intent past turn 1 was
lethal.

Tool defects hit this run twice: a deck-select UI bug blocked the first
Luminous Choir pick until a mid-run mod rebuild, and AllEnemies potions
silently no-op'd when given a target_combat_id (fixed in the mod after the
run; see What went poorly).

## What went well

- **Bag of Marbles + DISMANTLE**: the run's engine. Combat-start Vulnerable
 meant DISMANTLE+ hit for 24-30 at 1 energy from floor 11 onward; elites and
 the boss melted on burst turns (UPPERCUT+DISMANTLE = 43 dmg vs Byrdonis).
- **Bash → DISMANTLE sequencing from floor 1**: even before the relic, Bash
 (8 + 2 Vulnerable) followed by DISMANTLE (double hit × Vulnerable) was 32
 dmg for 3 energy — killed floor-2's 40-HP beetle in two turns.
- **Shop line at 245g**: Bag of Marbles (161g) + remove Spore Mind (75g) spent
 nearly all gold on exactly what the deck wanted; no wasted purchases.
- **Event card-removal value**: Luminous Choir "reach into the flesh" removed
 2 Strikes for a curse — net deck quality up once the curse was excised at
 the shop 5 floors later.
- **Burning Pact as curse/status insurance**: Pact exhausted junk and drew 2;
 it fed on slime SLIMED cards and would have fed Spore Mind if kept.
- **Boss kill from 1 HP side**: the vuln burst line (Thunderclap → DISMANTLE
 30 → cleanup Strike) finished Vantom over turns 4-8 while taking only one
 more hit — the deck's damage was never the problem.

## What went poorly

- **HP floor ignored when spending the last heal**: rest sites went Smith
 (floor 8, DISMANTLE+) then Heal (floor 15, 50→74). Correct individually,
 but the boss's 21+21+28 pattern still left 1 HP — no plan existed for
 "survive Act 2 floor 1 at low HP" (e.g. Pantograph at 207g in the same shop
 would have healed 25 at boss start; we skipped it for Marbles+removal and
 never regretted Marbles, but the boss-heal line was information we had and
 did not act on).
- **Block density never addressed**: 4 Defends all run; ARMAMENTS appeared
 twice (floor-7 reward, floor-11 shop at 51g) and was declined both times
 for offense. A 5-block+upgrade card would not have saved Act 2 floor 1
 alone, but more block pickups earlier change boss math (28-dmg Dismember
 turns).
- **Jungle Maze JOIN_FORCES at 63 HP**: followed the memory heuristic "JOIN
 mid/low HP, SOLO only over 60" — 63 HP barely qualified for SOLO, chose
 JOIN for +50g and kept HP. Defensible pre-boss, but the heuristic should
 weigh "boss next / no shop sink for gold" higher than a raw 60-HP line
 (see references update).
- **Potion of Binding misuse cost a boss turn**: passed
 `target_combat_id=1` on an AllEnemies potion; use was accepted
 (ok=True) but did nothing and did not consume the potion. Turn 3's plan
 assumed Weak+Vuln and under-damaged. Correct usage (no target) worked
 on turn 5/7. Mod fixed post-run.
- **Deck-select screen deadlock mid-event**: Luminous Choir's 2-card removal
 screen never registered choices — FindAll&lt;NCardHolder&gt;, which includes preview/ghost holders, so
 `choose` Pressed dead nodes. Fixed live to NGridCardHolder (the game's own
 AutoSlay path), rebuilt the mod, restarted, continue_run — event state
 restored cleanly.
- **First boss damage race mis-sized**: turn 7 DISMANTLE+TWIN_STRIKE left
 Vantom at 3 HP instead of killing (DISMANTLE dealt 24 not 30 — Vulnerable
 had ticked to a weaker state than assumed); the extra boss turn cost 21 HP
 and put us at 1 HP going into Act 2. Kill-threshold checks should re-read
 live HP after each card inside the batch, not assume table damage.
