## What went poorly

- **Act2 Boss block-density gap at strength-escalation (the death)**: Crusher's STR climbed 2→4→8 across the fight; BUG_STING scaled 6×2 → 9×2 → 14×2. The defensive package (Impervious once, Defend face-5, Rage+Fan procs ~13/turn) covered 12–20 incoming until T8, when 28 arrived on a hand with no Rage and Impervious already exhausted. Reference observation for future runs: a boss whose strength escalates needs either a Weak-immune block loop, a second big-block card, or a damage race that finishes before STR-8 turns — this deck had the race shape (Rocket dead on schedule) but not the wall for phase 2.
- **Process debt — chained bash calls**: this run repeatedly used multi-card bash chains; each one where indices shifted produced a fail-loud rejection (Defend with target, Ritual with target, IronWave without target, out-of-range index) and one **unplanned Whirlwind play** in the Decimillipede fight (third chained call hit Whirlwind at index 0 instead of the intended card) that put id2 into DEAD state early. The fight was recovered via same-window doctrine, but the lesson stands: one act per call, re-read between calls — chains still reindex the hand.
- **Forgotten Ritual + Drum live negative**: archive suggested Drum's self-exhaust might satisfy Ritual's "another card exhausted" condition — live T4 showed **no refund** (energy 2→1 after Ritual, no +3 logged). The working enabler is Burning Pact (explicit exhaust of another card). Ritual stayed dead weight most of the run.
- **Rage display vs Vital Spark**: archive listed Rage under "powers are safe" for zero-skill fights; live prism fight applied TAINTED on Rage play. Display `card_type: power` does not guarantee skill-tax immunity — verify live, don't trust the type label.
- **Surrounded facing micro-rules cost damage**: every targeted play flips facing (live: targeting Crusher dropped THRASH to 12; the very next Bash on Rocket flipped it back to 18). Mixed-target turns paid the flank tax repeatedly. Facing should be part of the per-turn plan, not an afterthought — pick one side per turn when possible.
- **Gold stranded at Act2 end**: 120g unspent — Act2 shops sat at (2,1) [sunk 282g] and (12,1) [sunk 147g], but the post-(12,1) spine (elite→rest→boss) had no further shop. Structural, noted in the draft, still worth flagging: the (10,6) shop on the right spine was never reachable from the chosen line.
- **Deck size at boss gate**: 29 cards with two Bludgeons, double Tremble, double Rage — draw variance showed at the lethal turn (no Rage, no Impervious, single Defend). The Bludgeon#2 and second Tremble picks added burst redundancy but diluted block-consistency draws.
