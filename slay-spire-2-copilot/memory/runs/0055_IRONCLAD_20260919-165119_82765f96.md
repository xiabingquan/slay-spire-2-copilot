## What went well

- **Glam (SILKEN_TRESS) live rule discovered**: the enchanted card
  double-settles on its **first play each combat**, single-settles afterwards.
  Glam Shrug = 16 block + 2 draw on the opening turn it is played — the
  strongest defensive open available to this deck. Run-49's "Glam first-strike
  double settle" note is refined by this run's data (T1 elite Shrug 16 vs T5
  same-combat Shrug 8).
- **Rage+ × attack-density block engine live**: 0-cost Rage+ (3→5 block per
  attack) turned Strike-heavy turns into block turns — T6 wave turn: Rage+ +
  3 Strikes = 15 block while dealing 18. With Orichalcum as the empty-block
  safety net (live +6 on free-window turns), the pair answers the classic
  "attack-heavy hand, no block card" death mode.
- **IronWave+ as attack-class block**: 7 block + 7 damage (×1.5 under vuln →
  10 live) is the hybrid the deck needed; it killed an Inklet through a
  Slippery charge and traded evenly in the wave.
- **Central-rest corridor draft executed 1:1**: two smiths (Rage+, IronWave+)
  before Elite ①, treasure en route, 80/80 entry, potion belt full. The
  route-geometry lesson (run-35/44 class) is holding.
- **Reference doctrines that landed live**: Fuzzy Wurm inhale kill-clock
  (curated, T3 kill), Shrinker ×0.70 floor math (Bash 8→5), Inklet Slippery
  charge drain (first hit 1), INFESTED wave-spawn (4 Wrigglers same frame,
  combat blocked until wave resolves), Infection ticks blockable (block
  absorbed 3-damage ticks repeatedly).
- **Potion category line**: Weak Potion on the parasite's Lash turn cut live
  intent 4×4 → 3×4 (16→12); Swift Potion drew 3 out of an Infection-clogged
  hand on wave T8 — both spent on the fight they were carried for.
- **Fail-loud held**: one stale-index attempt (Infection card via old hand
  print) rejected with HasUnplayableKeyword — no wrong card played.

## What went poorly

- **Wave kill-clock vs deck shape**: 4 Wrigglers × ~19 HP = 77 HP of bodies
  behind a status-dilution tax, against a 14-card single-target deck (5
  Strikes, Bash+, Pillage, IronWave+; no AoE). The parasite died on T4 but the
  wave ran to T13 — Wriggler Strength stacked +2 per wriggle cycle (live:
  bite 6 → 8 → 10 → 12 → 14) while Infection cards diluted every redraw.
  Suggestion for future runs: INFESTED-class elites are an AoE/kill-timer
  check at draft time — Whirlwind/Foul/Breakthrough-class, or end-of-turn
  tick damage, is the natural wave answer; without one, entry HP wants to be
  higher than 80/80's margin proved to be.
- **Final-turn hand was terminal**: 3 playable cards across two draws
  (Pillage + Shrug, both drawing Infection); last Wriggler at 3/21 lived
  because the drawn cards kept being status. The deck had the damage in it —
  it was not in the hand. Advisory: Infection fights favor exhaust/removal
  (Burning Pact-class was offered at run-55's own second card reward and
  declined for Pillage's race value — the wave fight is the counter-case).
- **Card pick reconsidered**: Pillage (draw-while-attacking) was taken over
  Burning Pact (exhaust a card, draw 2) at the inklet reward. Pillage pulled
  weight in hallway races, but Burning Pact would have thinned Infection out
  of the wave fight's hands. Reference only — live deck shape decides.
- **104g died unspent** — drafted consequence of the central corridor (no
  shop after (3,6) with purchasing power). Noted as tradeoff data, not a
  process miss.
