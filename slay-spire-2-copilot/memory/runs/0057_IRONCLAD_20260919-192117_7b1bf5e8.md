## Run review

The first A2 run was a doctrinal showcase: every Act1/Act2 elite and the Act1 boss died to a references-backed playbook rather than brute force — SHRIEK crossing stun (Terror Eel), HARDENED_SHELL pacing (Skulking Colony), garden-eel quartet AoE+kill-order, zero-skill discipline (Infested Prism), IMBALANCED full-block stun (Bowlbug Rock), Slumber no-chip (Slumbering Beetle), REATTACH same-window (Decimillipede), and SANDPIT FE-extension (The Insatiable, partially). The deck engine — FNP×Second Wind×Drum exhaust spine, Bludgeon+ (42+Sharp2+Cannon = 47-class live hits), Perfected Strike, Feed MaxHP rail (2 Fatals), Chosen Cheese (+1 MaxHP/combat) — matured exactly as the win-run references prescribe.

The death is a race-deficit death, not a doctrine death: 321 HP boss vs a deck averaging ~20-25 dmg/turn without its big hitters in hand, with all potions spent earlier in Act2 (correctly — they saved the three elite fights) and the Inferno tax quietly eating the last HP buffer. The HP wall killed at the boss gate, but this time after every gate doctrine item (rest-heal to 53/97, FE extensions, Weak-cut via Uppercut) was executed.

## What went poorly

- **Act2 Boss race deficit**: 20-25 dmg/turn average vs 321 HP needed ~13 turns; Sandpit+attrition budget allowed ~7-8. Missing piece: no energy relic beyond Prismatic+TeaSet T1 spike, and no Weak-immune engine (Inferno procced only via its own −1 tax = 9/turn, since Breakthrough/Feed rarely appeared in the same hands as the big hitters).
- **Potion economy at the boss gate**: all potions (Regen, Stable Serum, Blood, Powdered Demise, Weak, Heart of Iron, Power) were spent across Act2 elites/corridor — correct per doctrine but left the boss fight with zero reserve. The "≥1 potion at boss gate" checklist item was paid for by the three elite wins; next time weigh one corridor potion save.
- **TOXIC miscalculation (mite fight T2)**: plan said "leave 1 Toxic, pay 5 EoT tax" but BOTH Toxic cards sat unplayed — 10 unblocked damage after BITE ate the block. The tax math must count every Toxic in hand, not one.
- **Process debt — reward auto-claims**: chained choose(0) calls auto-picked CINDER (Act1), KINGLY_KICK and THUNDERCLAP (Act2) without evaluating alternatives (Pommel Strike, Adrenaline, Compile Driver/Haze went unread). The "read options before choosing" rule needs single-call reward claiming enforced.
- **Smith index drift (7,6 rest)**: intended Feed+ (Fatal +3→+4 MaxHP), hit INFERNO+ instead (index 21 vs 19). Inferno+ (Amount 9) proved its worth later (Decimillipede same-window, boss tax chip) — a lucky wrong turn — but the drift pattern recurred all run.
- **Draft geometry miss (Act1)**: route draft wrote "(5,4)M→(6,5)Rest" but map connectivity locked (5,4)→(6,4) Monster — the rest-before-Elite① geometry required the (5,5) fork I didn't take. - **Inferno tax as death-adjacent**: −1 HP/turn unblockable ate the final buffer in both the millipede fight (survived at 21) and contributed at the boss (13 HP floor). Engine tax must be budgeted every turn it's in play — run-41/42 lesson restated live.
