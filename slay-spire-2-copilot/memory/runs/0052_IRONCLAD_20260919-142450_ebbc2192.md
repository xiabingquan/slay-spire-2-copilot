The potion was consumed with zero effect; T8 collapsed to a 1-card Defend line under the bridge's `no(None)` gate. Forward suggestion: before any cleanse-class potion play, check whether the target tax's id carries `_POWER` — if yes, expect no cure.
- **Boss HP-floor accounting**: entering at 50/80 (skull sacrificed for buffer — the correct call vs 26) still left no margin once Plow escalated to 24–26/turn. The engine-setup turns (T3 Rupture+Mantle, T4 Rupture#2+FB) bought the race package at 15+2 HP each while hand block was thin. Suggestion: when racing a ≤150-class stun threshold, price each setup turn at (enemy intent − available block) + (engine self-damage) before choosing the engine line over a damage line.

Suggestion: count Mantle+BL+BT per-turn HP cost into the boss survival budget alongside enemy intents, and treat "HP ≤ projected engine tax" as a hard stop for self-damage plays.
- **Fairy spent pre-boss**: the double-Nibbit fight consumed the death-insurance charge; the boss corridor then had zero revive buffer. Suggestion: Fairy-class potions read as boss-corridor reserve — hall-fight lethal lines at 1HP may deserve a heavier block line instead.
