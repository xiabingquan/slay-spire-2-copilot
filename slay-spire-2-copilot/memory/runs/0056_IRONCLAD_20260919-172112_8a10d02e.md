## What went well

- **Three boss kills in one run, zero SL**: Ink Phantom T8, KaiserCrab T9,
  Queen+Torchhead T9. The self-harm → Rupture strength engine scaled through
  all three acts without a single save/load.
- **MaxHP stacking package live**: CHOSEN_CHEESE (proc every combat) + FEED
  (Fatal triggers) + LOOMING_FRUIT +31 + BIG_MUSHROOM +20 + STRAWBERRY +7 =
  HP 80→160 by Act 3. The doubled HP pool absorbed boss tax fights that would
  have killed at 80HP.
- **BoomingConch Neow value confirmed**: elite combat start draw 2 + 1 energy —
  the engine came online faster in every elite fight.
- **PaelsFlesh energy engine**: from T3 onward +1 energy/turn — the self-harm
  engine needed that extra energy to play Bloodletting + Hemokinesis + attack
  in the same turn.
- **Run-44 Queen doctrine live**: Torchhead killed T6 first (minion priority),
  then Queen T9. The "threat source first" rule from run-44's post-mortem held.
- **KaiserCrab rocket-first doctrine**: rocket 199 killed before crusher 209 —
  consistent with historical kill-window doctrine.
- **Gold efficiency**: three shops, nearly all gold spent (Act1 281g, Act2
  163g, Act3 330g = 774g total spent). Act3 shop drained 331→1.
- **Inferno AoE value**: Act 2 exoskeleton trio (HARD_TO_KILL:9) and Act 3
  scroll fights — Inferno's self-damage tick + AoE damage was the wave answer
  that run-55 lacked.
- **Symbiote Corrupted enchant**: Bludgeon+ enchanted with Corrupted — the
  1.5× multiplier on an already-upgraded 3-cost attack was the Act 3 boss
  burst piece.

## What went poorly

- **KaiserCrab HP margin**: 15/96 at fight end — self-harm tax + boss damage
  nearly ended the run at Act 2. The engine pays HP every turn; against a
  dual-body boss with 408 total HP, the tax compounded. Advisory: self-harm
  engines against high-HP bosses want a healing plan (rest before, or
  Bloodletting to fuel without net HP loss when paired with Feed).
- **Act 1 Elite 旧日雕像 HP cost**: 52→34 entry-to-exit — the statue fight
  was the run's biggest single HP tax outside bosses. SLOW_POWER + multi-hit
  intents drained through block.
- **Act 3 boss HP drop**: 150→26 over 9 turns — Queen's debuff chain (Weak +
  Puppet Strings-class) reduced output while Torchhead ramped. Won at 26/160,
  but the margin was thin for a 160HP pool.
- **BigMushroom −2 draw**: the +20 MaxHP was worth it, but T1 draw penalty
  cost tempo in every subsequent fight. Trade-off data for future runs.
