  at the biggest scale: 42 × 1.5 × 2 = 126 on Vantom T4 (exact). The counter is
  trackable from the compact state (`PEN_NIB:n`) — lining up the 10th attack as
  the Bludgeon turn is a repeatable planning target.
- **Very Hot Cocoa (Act 2 boon relic)** delivered unexpected value: +4 energy on
  turn 1 of every combat turned opening turns into full combo turns
  (Bloodletting + Bludgeon + Unrelenting/Tremble all fit). Suggested as a
  high-priority Act 2 boon for combo decks — reference, live situation decides.
- **Rupture × Bloodletting/Blood Wall** engine went live mid-run (run 54 was its
  first live procs in this save's records): Bloodletting's −3 HP converted to +1
  Strength three times in the exoskeleton quartet fight, and Blood Wall's −2
  fired it again — the "self-damage as fuel" card pool now has its missing
  amplifier.
- **Strike Dummy (+3 to Strike-tagged, pre-multiplier)** and **Armaments
  (combat-scoped upgrade)** both delivered: Strikes resolved 9–16 class under
  Strength+Vuln, and in-combat Armaments upgrades (Defend+, Tremble+) were used
  on the turns they were drawn.
- **Mechanic-first reads paid off in every Act 1 fight**: Slippery charge-drain
  (Vantom), IMBALANCED full-block stun (BowlbugRock — 15/15 exact twice),
  HARD_TO_KILL pacing (exoskeletons), Slow cheap-first/big-last (Bygone Effigy),
  Territorial growth kill-clock (Byr Donis), INFESTED wave spawn (Phrog
  Parasite). No unfamiliar id informed a decision un-researched.
- **Route drafts executed**: Act 1 draft spine walked as written; the (9,6)
  fork was taken on the draft's own condition (HP ≥ 50%, potions full); gold
  sinks landed at the drafted shops (Act1 125/129 = 96.3%, Act2 224/236 =
  94.9%).
- **Parrying Shield + Gorget + Tuning Fork** formed a passive block-value layer:
  end-of-turn ≥10 block → 6 chip damage (multiple live procs), 4 plating every
  combat start, +7 block every 10 skills (several procs this run).

## What went poorly

- **Act 2 Elite ① entry HP (31/80 = 39%) after Louse Progenitor** is the
  decision-level loss to reconsider: the draft's own threshold logic (<50% →
  alternative spine / potions as reserve) had no alternative spine reachable
  from (12,4)'s children in Act 1, but in Act 2 the (6,5)/(6,6) fork DID offer
  Elite-vs-Monster choice and the shop at (6,6) was spent on damage relics
  (Strike Dummy + Bloodletting #2 = 224g) rather than the available sustain
  (Eternal Feather 225g was unaffordable alongside; Beetle Juice 98g was
  blocked by full potion slots). Suggestion for future runs: when sub-50% HP
  at a shop that precedes a forced/scheduled elite, consider freeing a potion
  slot for a mitigation-class potion (Beetle Juice −30% enemy attack for 4
  turns class) before buying pure damage.
- **Decimillipede REATTACH window never formed**: incoming scaled faster
  (segments' Bulk +2 Str each cycle → Writhe 7×2-class) than the deck could
  both block AND spread damage. The doctrine (keep all alive, drop all in one
  window) is sound but needs either an AoE instrument (none in deck — no
  Whirlwind/Foul-class) or higher block density. Advisory: AoE or
  end-of-turn-tick damage (Powdered Demise-class was in the (6,6) shop at 75g
  and went unbought) is the natural REATTACH answer; without one, entry HP
  needs to be near-full.
- **Headbutt retrieve index mistake** (Louse Progenitor T3): the discard overlay
  offered Finesse/BloodWall/…; index 0 was taken (Finesse) when index 2
  (Blood Wall) was the intended T4 block source — T4 then had to race without
  it. Advisory: when a retrieve decision is block-critical, name the card id
  and pick its index from the fresh overlay print, not from positional memory.
- **Stale-index and Self-card-with-target process debt** recurred ~10+ times
  this run (playing index N from a previous hand print; passing target_combat_id
  to Blood Wall/Second Wind/One-Two/Armaments). Every instance was intercepted
  by fail-loud; none caused a wrong card to be played. Advisory: the one-card-
  per-call rule is holding the line, but re-reading the hand print before each
  call (not only after rejections) is the standing process suggestion.
- **Block-on-free-window waste (Bygone Effigy T2 analog)**: this specific
  pattern (banking block on a no-attack intent turn) did not recur — noted as
  an internalized lesson, not a loss.
