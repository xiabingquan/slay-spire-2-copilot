# Combat principles (lessons)

Experience is written as Markdown unordered-list items (`- item`); entries distilled from phased reflections carry their date.

# HP economy

- (2026-09-19) HP exchange rate shifts by turn: cheap early/low-threat, priceless on razor turns and boss-tax windows — weigh each payment against what the turn buys (advisory)
- (2026-09-18) The HP-economy wall is the historical top death cause: act-wide tax stacking + act-switch full-HP assumption + no-rest-before-elite geometry

# Engine online

- (2026-09-19) Opening turns: engines/powers online outrank marginal damage or minor HP savings — early HP for engine pieces is often the right trade
- (2026-09-19) Count self-damage engines as '-N HP per turn' inside boss/elite survival budgets

# Block density and lethal turns

- (2026-09-18) Boss block-density gaps are a frequent death cause: name block sources vs the boss intent profile before entering
- (2026-09-19) Post-play verification: after the key block card on a lethal turn, re-read state and confirm the block number before end_turn

# Race windows and tax management

- Terminology: tax = a forced, recurring cost levied per turn / per card / per card-class in a fight (vs one-time damage) — power taxes (enemy powers charging HP or play-count each turn, e.g. RINGING/Disintegration/Chains), status-card taxes (deck/hand pollution, e.g. Beckon/Dazed), skill taxes (charged per card-class, e.g. Vital Spark), engine self-damage taxes (your own engine's per-turn HP cost, e.g. Inferno ticks/self-damage fuel); race window = the time to finish before the tax crushes you

- (2026-09-18) Stat-drain bosses: the free window is a timer — burst resources must land inside it
- (2026-09-19) Weak-immune damage packages (power/relic ticks, poison — at least two kinds) belong in boss reference lines
- (2026-09-19) Power-class taxes (RINGING/Disintegration class) are NOT cleared by Cure All — do not reserve potion slots for them

# Mechanics lookup and live-first

- (2026-09-18) Full pre-fight read: enemy move_graph + POWER:amount + full map rows; a one-line data stub = unknown — research before deciding
- (2026-09-19) When archive and live conflict, live wins; play every bridge-ok card; lookup new player-side powers/debuffs on the turn they land
- (2026-09-19) Known bosses' secondary kill moves (EXPLODE class) also enter reference lines — with block-value numbers

# In-fight process discipline

- (2026-09-18) One card per call + full state re-read before every act call — the only defense against silent wrong-cards; never chain play+end_turn
- (2026-09-19) Process debt shifted from 'wrong cards' to 'missing plays': verify key block/damage landed between calls
- (2026-09-19) After reward-screen picks, verify names against player.deck — printed order is not applied order
