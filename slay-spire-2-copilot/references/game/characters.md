# Characters

Five playable characters. Starter loadouts cross-checked against game data
(this machine runs v0.107.1) and community databases; database counts from
spire-codex.com.

- IRONCLAD — 80 HP. Starter relic: BURNING_BLOOD (heal 6 HP at end of
  combat). Starter deck: Strike ×5, Defend ×4, Bash. Card pool: ~87.
  Archetype: Strength stacking, heavy single hits (Bludgeon), block-for-damage
  payoffs (Body Slam, Juggernaut), self-damage engines (Bloodletting,
  Offering, Hemokinesis). Key observed cards: Demon Form, Whirlwind,
  Uppercut, Blood Wall, Second Wind.
- SILENT — 70 HP. Starter relic: RING_OF_THE_SNAKE (draw 2 at combat start).
  Starter deck: Strike ×5, Defend ×5, Neutralize, Survivor. Card pool: ~88.
  Signature keyword: Sly — a Sly card can be played for its Energy cost, or
  discarded to trigger its effect automatically for free. Archetypes: Poison
  (Noxious Fumes, Deadly Poison, Poisoned Stab), Shivs (0-cost attacks,
  Blade Dance, Infinite Blades), discard synergy (Calculated Gamble,
  Reflex, Tactician, Tough Bandages, Tingsha).
- DEFECT — 70 HP. Starter relic: CRACKED_CORE (Channel 1 Lightning at combat
  start). Starter deck: Strike ×4, Defend ×4, Zap, Dualcast. Card pool: ~88.
  Mechanic: Orbs — channel Lightning / Frost / Plasma / Dark into orb slots;
  each orb has a passive (triggered) and an evoke (on-leave) effect; Focus
  scales orb output (Data Disk: +1 Focus at combat start). Archetypes: orb
  stacking (Capacitor, Loop, Multi-Cast), zero-cost/claw, power spam
  (Creative AI, Echo Form).
- NECROBINDER — 66 HP. Starter relic: BOUND_PHYLACTERY (Summon 1 at the
  start of your turn). Starter deck: Strike ×4, Defend ×4, Bodyguard,
  Unleash. Card pool: ~88. Mechanic: bone tokens, summons (Minion keyword —
  summoned creatures fight alongside you), Doom (stacking death-clock on
  enemies; Undying Sigil relic interacts), sacrifice loops (Minion
  Sacrifice, Death's Door).
- REGENT — 75 HP. Starter relic: DIVINE_RIGHT (gain 3 stars ★ at combat
  start). Starter deck: Strike ×4, Defend ×4, Falling Star, Venerate. Card
  pool: ~88. Mechanic: stars (★) as a combat resource spent by decree-style
  cards; court attendant / Minion payoffs (Vitruvian Minion doubles Minion
  card damage and block), Forge keyword (Fencing Manual: Forge 10 at combat
  start), prestige scaling.

Shared run structure: 3 acts per run, map with branching paths (monster /
elite / boss / event / shop / rest / treasure); card rewards after combat;
Neow boon at run start; rest sites offer Rest and Smith plus character-specific
options. In state snapshots the character field uses the ids IRONCLAD,
SILENT, DEFECT, NECROBINDER, REGENT.
